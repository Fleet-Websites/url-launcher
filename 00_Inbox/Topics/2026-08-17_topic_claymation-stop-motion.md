**Practical note:** stop motion does not require dedicated software at the
beginner level. A phone camera, a lamp, and a free video editor that can import
image sequences (Kdenlive, Shotcut, DaVinci Resolve) is enough to make the first
film. Dragonframe and Boats Animator add live view, onion-skinning, and frame
interval control — useful once the technique is proven.
