### Risograph

Risograph is the most distinctive process for indie publishing and zine work: it
produces textured, slightly misregistered layers in a limited ink range that cannot
be faked by digital means. Its ecological footprint is lower than laser printing.

**The practical blocker:** a Riso printer costs several thousand dollars. The
standard workaround is sending files to a riso print shop or studio. The Reed and
RISOTTO resources cover both running the machine yourself and preparing artwork for
a third-party printer.
